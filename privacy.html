TrailLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

TrailLog ("the App", "we", "us") is a logging and reference app for amateur ("ham") radio operators, built by an independent developer. This policy explains what data the App handles, where it's stored, and what (if anything) leaves your device.

TrailLog does not operate its own servers or accounts. There is no TrailLog backend collecting your data. Your log, contacts, and notes are stored on your device, with optional backup to your own iCloud account. The App does contact a number of third-party amateur radio and space-weather data services on your behalf, listed below.

1. Information you provide

You may enter the following information directly into the App. It is stored locally on your device:

  • Your callsign and station details (Preferences)
  • QSO (contact) logs: callsigns, names, locations, signal reports, notes, timestamps, and activation references (SOTA/POTA/WWFF etc.)
  • Your Contacts list, additional callsigns, and remarks
  • Custom map boundaries, imported GPX tracks, and watch-list entries
  • Optional third-party account credentials or API keys you choose to enter (see Section 3)

None of this is transmitted to us. We do not have a server, and we cannot see or access your log, contacts, or notes.

2. Location data

If you grant permission, the App uses your device's location (via Apple's Core Location, "When In Use" only) to:

  • Centre the map on your position and calculate distances to references, spots, and contacts
  • Populate propagation calculations with your current location as a starting point

Location is requested on demand (e.g. when you tap Refresh), not tracked continuously or in the background. Your location is not stored by us or sent to any analytics service. It may be sent to certain third-party services listed in Section 3 (e.g. propagation or nearby-activation lookups) as part of the request those features make on your behalf.

3. Third-party services the App connects to

Several features work by querying public amateur radio and space-weather data services directly from your device. When you use these features, your device communicates directly with the relevant third party, and that organisation's own privacy policy governs how they handle the request (typically your callsign, an API key or login you've provided, and/or your approximate location).

Any username, password, or API key you enter for these services is stored only in your device's Keychain (encrypted, device-only) and is sent directly to that service, never to us.

4. iCloud backup

If enabled in Preferences, the App periodically saves a backup of your log, archive, and contacts to your own iCloud Drive (under Files → TrailLog → Backups). This is Apple's iCloud service, governed by Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to it. You can disable this at any time in Preferences.

5. Crash reports

If the App crashes, a diagnostic report is generated and stored locally on your device only. Nothing is sent automatically. You can review the report and choose to email it to the developer yourself via the Mail app. This is entirely optional and requires you to tap Send.

6. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

TrailLog does not use analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or any form of cross-app tracking. We do not sell or share your data with advertisers, and we have no mechanism to do so even if we wanted to, since we don't collect it in the first place.

7. Data deletion

Since your data lives on your device, you're in full control:

  • Delete individual QSOs, contacts, or backups from within the App
  • Clear any saved credentials or API keys in Preferences
  • Deleting the App from your device removes all locally stored data
  • Deleting your iCloud backup files removes those independently, via the Files app

8. Children's privacy

TrailLog is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and since the App has no server-side data collection, no personal information is retained by us regardless of age.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if a new feature changes how data is handled. The "Last updated" date above will reflect any changes. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or how TrailLog handles data can be sent to: traillog@outlook.com

This policy describes TrailLog's data practices as of the date above.
